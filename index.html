About this workspace

Architecture through implementation.

These projects start with a business problem rather than a technology demonstration. Each implementation is used to test architectural assumptions, expose engineering tradeoffs, and understand how a design would need to evolve for production.

The goal is not to present one technology as the answer, but to make the reasoning behind the architecture visible and defensible.
